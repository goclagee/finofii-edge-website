# Complete Guide to Monthly Close Process

A solid monthly close process is the foundation of financial clarity. When done right, it gives you real-time visibility into your business performance and keeps you audit-ready year-round.

## Why Monthly Close Matters

Without a structured close process, financial data becomes unreliable. Decisions made on outdated or incorrect numbers can cost your business significantly.

**Benefits of a tight close process:**
- Accurate financials available within 5-10 business days of month-end
- Fewer surprises during tax season
- Better cash flow visibility
- Investor-ready reporting at any time

## The 10-Day Close Timeline

### Days 1-3: Data Gathering

1. Import all bank and credit card transactions
2. Collect outstanding invoices and receipts
3. Confirm payroll has been processed and recorded
4. Record any manual journal entries

### Days 4-6: Reconciliation

1. Reconcile all bank accounts to statements
2. Reconcile credit cards
3. Review accounts payable — verify all bills are recorded
4. Review accounts receivable — confirm customer payments

### Days 7-8: Adjustments

1. Record accrued expenses
2. Amortize prepaid expenses
3. Record depreciation
4. Adjust inventory (if applicable)

### Days 9-10: Review & Reporting

1. Review P&L against budget and prior month
2. Review Balance Sheet for unusual balances
3. Generate management reports
4. Document any notable variances

## Tools That Help

- **QuickBooks Online** or **Xero** for general ledger
- **Ramp** or **Brex** for automated expense categorization
- **Mercury** for startup banking with clean integrations

## Common Pitfalls

- Waiting too long to start (aim for day 1 after month-end)
- Not having a checklist (use our template!)
- Skipping reconciliation steps under time pressure
- Not documenting adjustments with supporting detail

## Getting Started

Start with the basics: bank reconciliation and a simple checklist. As your process matures, add more sophisticated steps like variance analysis and budget comparison.
