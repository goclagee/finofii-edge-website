# How to Choose the Right Accounting Software in 2024

Picking the right accounting software sets the foundation for everything that follows — bookkeeping efficiency, reporting quality, and integration capabilities.

## Decision Framework

Consider these factors when evaluating options:

1. **Business size and complexity** — Solo founder vs. multi-entity operation
2. **Industry-specific needs** — Inventory tracking, project accounting, MRR
3. **Integration ecosystem** — Banks, payment processors, payroll
4. **Scalability** — Will it grow with you through Series A and beyond?
5. **Cost** — Monthly fees plus add-on costs

## QuickBooks Online

**Best for:** Established small businesses, CPA-friendly workflows

- Most widely used platform in the US
- Strong ecosystem of third-party integrations
- Robust reporting and customization
- CPA/bookkeeper collaboration features built-in

**Limitations:** Interface can feel cluttered; advanced features require higher-tier plans.

## Xero

**Best for:** Startups, international businesses, clean UX preference

- Beautiful, modern interface
- Unlimited users on all plans
- Strong multi-currency support
- Excellent API for custom integrations

**Limitations:** Fewer US-specific features; smaller add-on marketplace than QuickBooks.

## Ramp (Corporate Card + Expense Management)

**Best for:** Expense management, real-time spend visibility

- Automated receipt matching and categorization
- Real-time spend controls and budgets
- Direct accounting software integrations
- No personal credit check required

**Limitations:** Not a full accounting platform; best used alongside QuickBooks or Xero.

## Our Recommendation

For most growing US businesses, we recommend **QuickBooks Online** as the core platform, paired with **Ramp** for expense management and spend control. This combination gives you robust general ledger capabilities with modern automation on the spending side.

If you're operating internationally or prefer a more developer-friendly platform, **Xero** is an excellent alternative.
