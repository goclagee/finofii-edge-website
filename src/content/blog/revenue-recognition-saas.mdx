# Revenue Recognition for SaaS: ASC 606 Simplified

Revenue recognition is one of the most common pain points for SaaS companies. With ASC 606 in full effect, understanding how to properly recognize subscription revenue is critical for audit-readiness and investor confidence.

## The Five-Step Model

ASC 606 introduces a five-step framework for recognizing revenue:

1. **Identify the contract** — A signed subscription agreement with a customer
2. **Identify performance obligations** — Software access, support, onboarding, etc.
3. **Determine transaction price** — Monthly or annual subscription fees
4. **Allocate the price** — Distribute across performance obligations
5. **Recognize revenue** — As obligations are satisfied over time

## Key Considerations for SaaS

### Subscription vs. Usage-Based

For flat-rate subscriptions, revenue is typically recognized ratably over the subscription period. Usage-based components may require variable consideration estimates.

### Multi-Element Arrangements

If your contract bundles software access with implementation services, you'll need to allocate the transaction price based on standalone selling prices.

> **Pro tip:** Establish standalone selling prices early, even if you don't currently sell components separately. This simplifies allocation later.

## Common Mistakes

- Recognizing annual prepayments upfront instead of ratably
- Failing to separate distinct performance obligations
- Not accounting for contract modifications properly
