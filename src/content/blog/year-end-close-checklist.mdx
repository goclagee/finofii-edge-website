# The Ultimate Year-End Close Checklist for Small Businesses

Year-end is when clean books really matter. Whether you're preparing for tax filing, investor reporting, or simply starting the new year right, this checklist covers everything.

## Week 1: Reconciliation

- [ ] Reconcile all bank accounts through December 31
- [ ] Reconcile credit card statements
- [ ] Clear outstanding checks and deposits in transit
- [ ] Verify petty cash balances

## Week 2: Accruals & Adjustments

- [ ] Record accrued expenses (wages, utilities, rent)
- [ ] Record accrued revenue (earned but not yet invoiced)
- [ ] Review prepaid expenses and adjust amortization
- [ ] Record depreciation entries for fixed assets

## Week 3: Review & Verify

- [ ] Review accounts receivable aging — write off uncollectibles
- [ ] Review inventory counts and adjust for shrinkage
- [ ] Verify loan balances match lender statements
- [ ] Confirm inter-company balances (if applicable)

## Week 4: Tax Prep & Reporting

- [ ] Gather 1099 information for contractors
- [ ] Review estimated tax payments made during the year
- [ ] Prepare preliminary P&L and Balance Sheet
- [ ] Document any unusual transactions for your CPA

## Tips for a Smooth Close

1. **Start early** — Don't wait until January to begin reconciliations
2. **Document everything** — Notes on adjustments save hours during audit
3. **Communicate** — Let your team know about deadlines for expense reports
